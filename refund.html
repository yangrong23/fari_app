Refund and Return Policy

This policy applies to one-time digital video purchases made through Medrix AI, operated under the Fari brand for customers in Brazil.

Last Updated

Last updated: September 1, 2026.

Effective date: September 1, 2026.

Please read this policy carefully before making a purchase.

1. Scope of This Policy

1.1 This policy applies to one-time purchases of digital video generation services provided by Medrix AI (Fari) to customers in Brazil.

1.2 Medrix AI (Fari) provides purely digital online services and does not involve the production or delivery of physical goods. Therefore, the term "return" in this policy refers specifically to cancellation of digital services and refund of fees, and does not apply to returning physical items.

1.3 Refund requests are handled only by email. Fari does not currently provide a separate support portal or third-party refund channel for this product.

1.4 This policy is intended to comply with the Brazilian Consumer Protection Code (Codigo de Defesa do Consumidor - Law No. 8.078/1990). If this policy conflicts with mandatory local law, the applicable local law will prevail.

2. When Refunds Apply

2.1 Refund Eligibility

A customer may request a refund if any of the following conditions apply. Where Brazilian law requires a full refund, Fari will refund the amount paid by the customer through the original payment channel where technically possible.

  • The customer requests cancellation within 7 calendar days after the one-time purchase under Article 49 of the Brazilian Consumer Protection Code.
  • A technical failure on our side prevents normal use of the service for more than 72 consecutive hours.
  • The core functions shown on the purchase page are materially inconsistent with the actual service.
  • An unauthorized duplicate charge occurs.

2.2 One-Time Digital Purchases

Each paid video is a one-time digital purchase.

Because there is no unused service period after a one-time order, pro-rated refunds do not apply.

2.3 Non-Refundable Cases

We may refuse a refund request in the following cases:

  • The order is connected to fraud, abuse, misuse, or violation of the Terms of Service or Acceptable Use Policy.
  • The same payer has already received a refund for a similar order within the past 6 months.
  • The order was obtained through unofficial channels, including third-party purchasing or gift card redemption.
  • The refund request is made after the applicable 7-day withdrawal period and there is no technical or billing issue.
  • The generated video has already been downloaded, shared, or substantially used, unless required otherwise by applicable law.

4. How to Request a Refund

4.1 How to Request a Refund

Refund requests must be submitted by email only:

  • Email request: send an email to contact@medrixai.com.
  • Email subject: Solicitação de Reembolso - [order number or payer email].
  • Email body: payment receipt or invoice, order or transaction number if available, payer email, payment date, payment amount, and refund reason if the customer chooses to provide one.

4.2 Billing Information to Provide

  • The payment receipt, invoice, or processor receipt email.
  • The payment processor order number beginning with "ch_", "pi_", or another transaction identifier if available.
  • The payer email, payment date, payment amount, currency, and the last four digits of the payment card if available.
  • If the refund request is based on a technical failure, screenshots or a description of the failure time should be included.

4.3 Customer Cooperation

  • If we request additional billing information, the customer must reply within 5 business days. Failure to reply within that period will be treated as abandonment of the request.
  • During refund review, please do not open a chargeback dispute through the payment processor or card issuer. Doing so may delay the review or require us to handle the matter through the dispute process.

5. Refund Processing Time

5.1 Review Time

We will complete the review within 3 business days after receiving a complete refund request, and we will notify the customer of the result by email.

5.2 Time to Receive the Refund

If approved, the refund will be returned through the original payment channel where technically possible.

Card or supported online paymentEstimated arrival time: 5-10 business days after refund submission by the processor.
  • The actual arrival time depends on the payment processor and the card issuer, and is outside our control.
  • If the refund has not arrived after 15 business days, contact contact@medrixai.com.
  • Refunds will be returned through the original payment channel where possible. The exchange rate, if any, follows the settlement rate used by the payment processor.

5.3 Service Termination

  • After approval, access to the refunded paid video file or related paid output may be disabled where technically possible.
  • Refund approval does not delete any local files already saved by the customer.

6. Refund Amount and Fees

6.1 For refunds required under Article 49 or other mandatory Brazilian consumer protection rules, Fari will refund the amount paid by the customer. Payment processing fees charged to Fari by Stripe or another payment provider will not be deducted from the statutory refund amount.

6.2 For service defects or delivery failures covered by Brazilian consumer law, Fari will follow the remedies required by law, including refunding the amount paid where that remedy applies.

6.3 Fari does not apply pro-rated refunds because purchases are one-time digital video orders.

6.4 The actual arrival time and any separate fees charged directly by a customer bank, card issuer, or currency conversion provider may be outside Fari's control.

7. Digital Delivery and No Physical Returns

7.1 Medrix AI (Fari) provides purely digital online services and does not produce, sell, or deliver physical goods.

7.2 No physical return, shipping label, or shipping address is required for a refund request.

7.3 Refund review is based on payment and billing records, generation status, and any technical evidence provided by the customer.

8. One-Time Orders Only

8.1 Paid videos are purchased as individual one-time digital orders.

8.2 There is no repeating charge for this product.

8.3 Customers will not be charged again unless they actively choose to purchase another video.

9. Statutory Consumer Rights in Brazil

9.1 This policy does not limit any statutory consumer rights under the Brazilian Consumer Protection Code (CDC / Law No. 8.078/1990) or other applicable laws.

9.2 Under Article 49 of the CDC, consumers may exercise the right of withdrawal within 7 calendar days after entering into the contract or receiving the product or service. If this right is exercised, amounts paid during the reflection period must be returned immediately and monetarily updated, as required by law.

9.3 Under Article 18 of the CDC, if a service has a quality issue or is inconsistent with its description, consumers may have the right to request a refund, replacement, or appropriate price reduction.

9.4 If local consumer protection law provides a longer refund period or more favorable refund conditions, the local law will apply.
