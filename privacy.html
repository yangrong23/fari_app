Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information fari collects, why it is used, and how users can contact us about privacy requests.

Information We Collect

  • Uploaded photos or media needed to generate previews and videos.
  • Prompt, template, and generation settings selected by the user.
  • Payment status and transaction metadata from payment providers.
  • Basic device, browser, and usage data for security, analytics, and reliability.

How We Use Information

We use information to provide video generation, process payments, troubleshoot errors, prevent abuse, improve product quality, and respond to user support requests.

Uploaded Media

Uploaded media is used to create the requested preview or final video. We do not sell uploaded personal media. Temporary files may be retained for a limited period for delivery, support, and abuse prevention.

Payments

Card and wallet payment details are processed by the payment provider. fari does not intentionally store full card numbers on its servers.

Cookies and Tracking

We may use cookies or similar technologies for essential site functionality, analytics, conversion measurement, and advertising performance. These tools may include Meta Pixel, Google Analytics, Google Ads conversion tags, and TikTok Pixel when enabled for the website.

User Rights

You can request access, correction, or deletion of personal information by contacting support. We may need to verify the request before acting on it.

Contact

Privacy requests can be sent through the Contact page.
