About Fari

Fari is an AI emotional video assistant operated by Medrix AI for people who want to turn a single meaningful photo into a personal video gift.

What We Do

fari helps users create short emotional videos for birthdays, family moments, weddings, graduations, pets, and other personal occasions. The product focuses on simple mobile-first creation: choose a template, upload a photo, preview, purchase, and download or share.
